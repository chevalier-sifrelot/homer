**Cassandra** is a Trojan princess, daughter of King Priam, renowned as the fairest of his daughters and remembered for her tragic fate after the fall of Troy.

## Appearances

Cassandra is mentioned briefly in the Iliad (Books XIII and XXIV) and in the Odyssey (Book XI), where her death is recounted by the ghost of Agamemnon.

## Personality

- **Renowned beauty**: she was sought after by suitors for her looks, as Othryoneus "sought Cassandra the fairest of Priam's daughters in marriage" (Iliad, Book XIII).
- **Devoted and watchful**: she was the first in Troy to notice her father's return with Hector's body, and "with a loud cry she went about the city" to alert everyone (Iliad, Book XXIV).
- **Tragic victim**: she met a violent end after the war, as Agamemnon's ghost recalls, "I heard Priam's daughter Cassandra scream as Clytemnestra killed her close beside me" (Odyssey, Book XI).
