# Phoenix

**Phoenix** is an aged warrior and orator who raised and tutored Achilles from boyhood, and who serves as a member of the embassy sent to persuade Achilles to return to battle.

## Appearances

Phoenix appears mainly in Book IX (the embassy to Achilles, where he delivers a long, emotional appeal), and is also mentioned in Book XVI (as captain of a company of Myrmidons), Book XIX, and Book XXIII (as umpire of the footrace).

## Personality

- **Devoted mentor and father figure to Achilles**: he was sent by Peleus specifically to raise the young Achilles — "he sent me with you to train you in all excellence of speech and action" (Iliad, Book IX) — and Achilles in turn calls him "old friend and father" (Iliad, Book IX).
- **Fiercely loyal, refusing to abandon Achilles**: he declares he would rather stay with Achilles than leave, even under threat — "Therefore, my son, I will not stay here without you- no, not though heaven itself vouchsafe to strip my years from off me" (Iliad, Book IX).
- **Persuasive and emotional in speech**: his plea to Achilles is marked by open weeping and heartfelt urging — "the old knight Phoenix in his great fear for the ships of the Achaeans, burst into tears and said, 'Noble Achilles...'" (Iliad, Book IX).

## Entourage

- Achilles
- Odysseus
- Ajax (son of Telamon)
