# Melanthius

**Melanthius** is the disloyal goatherd of Odysseus's household, son of Dolius, who abandons his master's cause to curry favor with the suitors and is killed for his treachery when Odysseus retakes his home.

## Appearances

Melanthius appears in the Odyssey, playing a notable role in Books XVII, XX, XXI, and XXII.

## Personality

- **Insolent and cruel**: he mocks the disguised Odysseus without provocation, sneering "Are you still here, stranger... to pester people by begging about the house? Why can you not go elsewhere?" (Odyssey, Book XX).
- **Treacherous toward his own household**: he threatens the loyal swineherd Eumaeus, boasting "Some day or other I will put you on board ship and take you to a foreign country, where I can sell you and pocket the money you will fetch" (Odyssey, Book XVII).
- **Openly sides with the suitors against his rightful master**: he wishes for Telemachus's death, saying "I wish I were as sure that Apollo would strike Telemachus dead this very day... as I am that Ulysses will never come home again" (Odyssey, Book XVII).
