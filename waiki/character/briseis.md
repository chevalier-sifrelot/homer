# Briseis

**Briseis** is a captive woman from Lyrnessus given to Achilles as a prize of war; Agamemnon's seizure of her from Achilles' tent triggers the quarrel that drives the plot of the Iliad.

## Appearances

Briseis appears in the Iliad, notably in Books I, II, IX, XIX, and XXIV.

## Personality

- **Reluctant captive attached to Achilles' household**: when the heralds led her away to Agamemnon, "the woman was loth to go" (Iliad, Book I).
- **Devoted in grief to those close to her**: mourning Patroclus, she cries, "Patroclus, dearest friend, when I went hence I left you living; I return, O prince, to find you dead" (Iliad, Book XIX).
- **Marked by profound personal loss**: she recalls "I saw him to whom my father and mother married me, cut down before our city, and my three own dear brothers perished with him on the self-same day" (Iliad, Book XIX).

## Entourage

- Achilles
- Patroclus
- Agamemnon
