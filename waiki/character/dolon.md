**Dolon** is a Trojan herald's son who volunteers to spy on the Achaean camp in exchange for Achilles' horses and chariot, only to be captured and killed by Diomed and Ulysses on his night mission.

## Appearances

Dolon appears in Book X of the Iliad, in the night raid episode where he is sent to spy on the Achaean ships and is caught and killed by Diomed and Ulysses.

## Personality

- **Greedy for a reward far beyond his station**: he demands Achilles' own horses and chariot as his price for scouting — "swear that you will give me the chariot, bedight with bronze, and the horses that now carry the noble son of Peleus. I will make you a good scout, and will not fail you" (Iliad, Book X).
- **Cowardly once caught**: he immediately begs for his life and offers ransom rather than fight — "Take me alive; I will ransom myself; we have great store of gold, bronze, and wrought iron" (Iliad, Book X).
- **Physically unimpressive but fast on his feet**: his one notable talent is speed, not strength or looks — "He was ill-favoured, but a good runner, and was an only son among five sisters" (Iliad, Book X).

## Entourage
