# Poseidon

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Kind** | God |
| **Origin** | The sea |
| **Gender** | Male |

**Poseidon** is the god of the sea and earthquakes, brother of Zeus, who secretly favours the Achaeans in the Iliad and becomes the relentless persecutor of Odysseus throughout the Odyssey after the blinding of his son Polyphemus.

## Appearances

Iliad: appears prominently, with a especially notable role in Books VII, VIII, XIII, and XX. Odyssey: appears throughout, with a especially notable role in Books I, V, IX, and XIII.

## Personality

- **Relentless and vengeful once wronged**: "to pity him except Neptune, who still persecuted him without ceasing" (Odyssey, Book I)
- **Takes cruel satisfaction in tormenting Odysseus with his mastery of the sea**: "'There now, swim up and down as you best can till you fall in with well-to-do people. I do not think you will be able to say that I have let you off too lightly.'" (Odyssey, Book V)
- **Proud and jealous of his own honour and glory among the gods**: "The fame of this wall will reach as far as dawn itself, and men will no longer think anything of the one which Phoebus Apollo and myself built with so much labour for Laomedon." (Iliad, Book VII)

## Entourage

- Zeus
- Odysseus
- Polyphemus
