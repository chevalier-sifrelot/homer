# Athena

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Kind** | God |
| **Origin** | [Mount Olympus](../location/mount-olympus.md) |
| **Gender** | Female |
| **Affiliated to** | Achaeans |

**Athena** is the grey-eyed goddess of wisdom and war, daughter of Zeus, who champions the Achaeans throughout the Iliad and personally guides Odysseus and his household through the Odyssey.

## Appearances

Iliad: appears throughout, with a especially notable role in Books I, IV, V, and XXII. Odyssey: appears throughout, with a especially notable role in Books I, II, XIII, and XXIV.

## Personality

- **A fierce and formidable warrior, unmatched even by the god of war himself**: "Pallas Minerva drove the spear into the pit of Mars's stomach" (Iliad, Book V)
- **A devoted champion who fills her favoured heroes with courage and glory**: "Then Pallas Minerva put valour into the heart of Diomed, son of Tydeus, that he might excel all the other Argives, and cover himself with glory." (Iliad, Book V)
- **Unrivalled in cunning, subtlety, and strategy**: "for diplomacy and subtlety have no equal among the gods." (Odyssey, Book XIII)

## Entourage

- Zeus
- Odysseus
- Telemachus
