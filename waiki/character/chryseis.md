# Chryseis

| | |

**Chryseis** is the daughter of Chryses, priest of Apollo at Chryse, taken captive and given to Agamemnon as a war-prize; Agamemnon's refusal to ransom her back to her father sets off the plague and the quarrel that opens the Iliad.

## Appearances

Chryseis appears only in the Iliad, entirely within Book I.

## Personality

- **Highly prized for her beauty and accomplishment**: Agamemnon declares "I love her better even than my own wife Clytemnestra, whose peer she is alike in form and feature, in understanding and accomplishments" (Iliad, Book I).
- **Taken as the choicest spoil of war**: after the sack of Thebe, the Achaeans "chose lovely Chryseis as the meed of Agamemnon" (Iliad, Book I).
- **A cherished daughter, restored to her family**: when Ulysses brings her home, "he gave the girl over to her father, who received her gladly" (Iliad, Book I).

## Entourage

- Chryses
- Agamemnon
- Ulysses
