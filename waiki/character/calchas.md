# Calchas

**Calchas** is the chief seer and augur of the Achaean army, renowned for his prophetic wisdom, who reveals the cause of the plague sent by Apollo and once foretold the length of the Trojan War.

## Appearances

Calchas appears notably in Book I, where he explains the plague afflicting the Achaean camp and clashes with Agamemnon over it, and Book II, where his old prophecy at Aulis about the length of the war is recalled.

## Personality

- **Wise and deeply knowledgeable**: he is introduced as "Calchas son of Thestor, wisest of augurs, who knew things past present and to come" (Iliad, Book I).
- **Cautious of powerful men's wrath**: before speaking he asks for protection, fearing retaliation — "for I know that I shall offend one who rules the Argives with might, to whom all the Achaeans are in subjection... Consider, therefore, whether or no you will protect me" (Iliad, Book I).
- **Ultimately honest and bold once reassured**: once Achilles promises to protect him, he speaks the truth without flinching — "Thereon the seer spoke boldly. 'The god,' he said, 'is angry neither about vow nor hecatomb, but for his priest's sake, whom Agamemnon has dishonoured'" (Iliad, Book I).

## Entourage

- Agamemnon
- Achilles
- Apollo
