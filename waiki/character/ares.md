# Ares

**Ares** is the god of war and slaughter, son of Zeus and Hera, who fights on the side of Troy in the Iliad and appears in the Odyssey as the lover of Aphrodite caught in Hephaestus's trap.

## Appearances

Iliad: appears prominently, with a especially notable role in Books V, XV, XX, and XXI. Odyssey: appears notably in Book VIII.

## Personality

- **Bloodthirsty and destructive, a bringer of ruin to cities**: "Mars, Mars, bane of men, blood-stained stormer of cities" (Iliad, Book V)
- **Quarrelsome and mischief-making, despised even by his own father**: "I hate you worst of all the gods in Olympus, for you are ever fighting and making mischief." (Iliad, Book V)
- **For all his terror in battle, he howls in pain like thousands of men when wounded**: "Mars roared as loudly as nine or ten thousand men in the thick of a fight" (Iliad, Book V)

## Entourage

- Aphrodite
- Zeus
- Hera
