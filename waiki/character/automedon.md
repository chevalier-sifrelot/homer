**Automedon** is the skilled charioteer and squire of Achilles, who drives Patroclus's chariot into battle and fights on with desperate fury after Patroclus is killed.

## Appearances

Automedon appears notably in Book IX, Book XVI (where he arms for battle alongside Patroclus and drives the immortal horses), Book XVII (where he fights to avenge Patroclus and nearly falls to Hector), Book XIX, Book XXIII, and Book XXIV.

## Personality

- **Highly trusted by Patroclus**: he is chosen above all others to drive the immortal horses into battle — "he was the man whom he held in honour next after Achilles, and on whose support in battle he could rely most firmly" (Iliad, Book XVI).
- **Grief-driven and fierce in battle**: after Patroclus's death he throws himself recklessly at the Trojans — "full of sorrow for his comrade, as a vulture amid a flock of geese. In and out, and here and there, full speed he dashed amid the throng of the Trojans" (Iliad, Book XVII).
- **Practical and self-aware of his limits**: he recognizes he cannot fight and drive alone, and calls on a comrade to take the reins — "Alcimedon, there is no one else who can control and guide the immortal steeds so well as you can, save only Patroclus- while he was alive" (Iliad, Book XVII).

## Entourage
