# Zeus

**Zeus** is the king of the gods and ruler of the sky, son of Cronus (Saturn), who presides over the councils of Olympus and holds ultimate power over the fates of gods and mortals alike in both epics.

## Appearances

Iliad: appears throughout, with a especially notable role in Books I, VIII, XIV, XV, XVI, XXII, and XXIV. Odyssey: appears throughout, with a especially notable role in Books I, V, and XXIV.

## Personality

- **Supremely powerful and dominant over the other gods**: "So far am I above all others either of gods or men." (Iliad, Book VIII)
- **Solemn and true to his word once given**: "I never recall my word, or deceive, or fail to do what I say, when I have nodded my head." (Iliad, Book I)
- **Impartial arbiter who weighs the fates of both sides**: "the sire of all balanced his golden scales, and put two fates of death within them, one for the Trojans and the other for the Achaeans." (Iliad, Book VIII)
