# Melantho

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Kind** | Human |
| **Origin** | [Ithaca](../location/ithaca.md) |
| **Gender** | Female |
| **Affiliated to** | Achaeans |

**Melantho** is a disloyal maidservant in Odysseus's household, daughter of Dolius, who was raised kindly by Penelope but betrays her mistress by taking Eurymachus as a lover and mocking the disguised Odysseus.

## Appearances

Melantho appears in the Odyssey, playing a notable role in Book XVIII.

## Personality

- **Ungrateful despite kindness shown to her**: though "brought up by Penelope, who used to give her toys to play with, and looked after her when she was a child... she showed no consideration for the sorrows of her mistress" (Odyssey, Book XVIII).
- **Contemptuous and mocking**: she scorns the disguised Odysseus, jeering "Poor wretch... are you gone clean out of your mind? Go and sleep in some smithy, or place of public gossips, instead of chattering here" (Odyssey, Book XVIII).
- **Persistently disrespectful**: she taunts him a second time, saying "Stranger, do you mean to plague us by hanging about the house all night and spying upon the women? Be off, you wretch" (Odyssey, Book XIX).

## Entourage

- Melanthius
- Eurymachus
- Penelope
