# Aeolus

**Aeolus** is the keeper of the winds, son of Hippotas, who lives with his family on the floating island of Aeolia and is a favorite of the gods, appointed by Jove as captain over the winds; he generously helps Odysseus on his way home before angrily turning him away when the winds go wrong a second time. He takes no side in the Trojan War, existing as a minor divine figure entirely outside that conflict.

## Appearances

Aeolus appears in the Odyssey in Book X, where Odysseus and his men stay on his island and receive the bag of winds, and he is mentioned again in Book XXIII.

## Personality

- **Hospitable and generous to guests**: "'Aeolus entertained me for a whole month asking me questions all the time about Troy... and when I said I must go, and asked him to further me on my way, he made no sort of difficulty'" (Odyssey, Book X).
- **Powerful, entrusted with divine authority**: "for Jove had made him captain over the winds, and he could stir or still each one of them according to his own pleasure" (Odyssey, Book X).
- **Harsh and unforgiving once he believes someone is cursed by heaven**: "'Vilest of mankind, get you gone at once out of the island; him whom heaven hates will I in no wise help. Be off, for you come here as one abhorred of heaven'" (Odyssey, Book X).

## Entourage

- Odysseus
- Zeus (Jove)
- His twelve children, six sons and six daughters, who feast together in his household
