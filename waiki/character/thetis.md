# Thetis

**Thetis** is a sea-goddess, the Nereid mother of Achilles, who intercedes with Zeus on her son's behalf and mourns throughout the Iliad the tragically short life fated for him.

## Appearances

Iliad: appears notably in Books I, XVIII, and XXIV. Odyssey: mentioned only in passing, without a direct role.

## Personality

- **A devoted, grieving mother who mourns her son's short, sorrowful fate**: "My son, woe is me that I should have borne or suckled you. Would indeed that you had lived your span free from all sorrow at your ships, for it is all too brief" (Iliad, Book I)
- **A confident and persuasive intercessor among the gods**: "nor do I doubt that I shall be able to persuade him." (Iliad, Book I)
- **Sorrowfully resigned to the tragedy awaiting her son**: "Then, my son, is your end near at hand- for your own death awaits you full soon after that of Hector." (Iliad, Book XVIII)

## Entourage
