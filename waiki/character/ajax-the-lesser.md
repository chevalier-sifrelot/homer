# Ajax the Lesser

**Ajax the Lesser** (son of Oileus) is the swift-footed leader of the Locrian contingent at Troy, a smaller but fierce warrior often paired in battle with Ajax the Great, whose boastfulness ultimately brings about his death on the voyage home.

## Appearances

In the Iliad he has notable roles in Books II, XIII, XIV, XVII, and XXIII (his quarrel with Idomeneus at the funeral games). In the Odyssey his fate at sea is recounted in Book IV.

## Personality

- **Swift and skilled with the spear**: introduced as "Ajax, the fleet son of Oileus... in use of the spear he excelled all the Hellenes and the Achaeans" (Iliad, Book II).
- **Quarrelsome and hot-tempered**: after he rudely interrupts Idomeneus at the funeral games, Idomeneus retorts, "Ajax you are an excellent railer, but you have no judgement... for you have a vile temper" (Iliad, Book XXIII).
- **Boastful to a fatal fault**: he survives being shipwrecked by Neptune but "ruined himself by boasting. He said the gods could not drown him even though they had tried to do so," prompting Neptune to kill him (Odyssey, Book IV).

## Entourage
