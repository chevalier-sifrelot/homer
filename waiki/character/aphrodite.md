# Aphrodite

| | |

**Aphrodite** is the goddess of love and beauty, daughter of Zeus, who protects Paris and Aeneas throughout the Iliad and appears in the Odyssey as the wife of Hephaestus caught in an affair with Ares.

## Appearances

Iliad: appears notably in Books III, V, and XIV. Odyssey: appears notably in Book VIII.

## Personality

- **A devoted, protective mother willing to brave the battlefield for her son**: "wounded me because I was bearing my dear son Aeneas, whom I love best of all mankind, out of the fight." (Iliad, Book V)
- **A goddess of love and seduction, ill-suited to the violence of war**: "can you not be contented with beguiling silly women? If you meddle with fighting you will get what will make you shudder at the very name of war." (Iliad, Book V)
- **Possesses an irresistible, magical charm and allure**: "into which all her charms had been wrought- love, desire, and that sweet flattery which steals the judgement even of the most prudent." (Iliad, Book XIV)

## Entourage

- Ares
- Paris
- Aeneas
