# Circe

**Circe** is a cunning enchantress-goddess who lives on the island of Aeaea, where she turns Odysseus's men into pigs before becoming his lover and advisor for a year, later guiding him on how to survive the perils that lie ahead. She stands entirely outside the Trojan War, belonging to neither the Achaean nor Trojan side.

## Appearances

In the Odyssey, Circe appears mainly in Book X, where Odysseus and his men land on Aeaea, and in Book XII, where she gives Odysseus detailed instructions on how to survive the Sirens, Scylla, and Charybdis. She is also referenced retrospectively in Books XI and XXIII.

## Personality

- **Dangerous and skilled in witchcraft**: Mercury warns Odysseus, "I will tell you of all the wicked witchcraft that Circe will try to practise upon you. She will mix a mess for you to drink, and she will drug the meal with which she makes it" (Odyssey, Book X).
- **Fierce but ultimately yielding when overpowered**: when her magic fails and Odysseus draws his sword, "she fell with a loud scream, clasped my knees, and spoke piteously, saying, 'Who and whence are you?'" (Odyssey, Book X).
- **Generous and caring once won over**: after freeing his men, "Circe herself was so sorry for them that she came up to me and said, 'Ulysses, noble son of Laertes, go back at once to the sea where you have left your ship'" (Odyssey, Book X).

## Entourage

- Odysseus
- Hermes (Mercury)
- Her nymph housemaids, "the children of the groves and fountains, and of the holy waters that run down into the sea"
