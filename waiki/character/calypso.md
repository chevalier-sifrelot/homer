# Calypso

**Calypso** is a nymph, daughter of Atlas, who lives alone on the island of Ogygia and keeps Odysseus with her for seven years, wishing to make him her immortal husband, until the gods order her to release him. She plays no part in the Trojan War itself, standing wholly apart from the Achaean and Trojan camps.

## Appearances

Calypso appears in the Odyssey primarily in Book V, where Hermes visits her with Jove's command to free Odysseus and she reluctantly helps him build a raft. She is also mentioned in Books I, IV, VII, IX, XII, and XXIII.

## Personality

- **Possessive and resentful of divine interference**: "Calypso trembled with rage when she heard this, 'You gods,' she exclaimed, 'to be ashamed of yourselves. You are always jealous and hate seeing a goddess take a fancy to a mortal man'" (Odyssey, Book V).
- **Genuinely affectionate toward Odysseus**: she recalls, "I got fond of him and cherished him, and had set my heart on making him immortal, so that he should never grow old all his days" (Odyssey, Book V).
- **Ultimately honest and compliant once resigned**: she swears to Odysseus, "May heaven above and earth below be my witnesses, with the waters of the river Styx... that I mean you no sort of harm" (Odyssey, Book V).

## Entourage

- Odysseus
- Hermes (Mercury)
- Zeus (Jove)
