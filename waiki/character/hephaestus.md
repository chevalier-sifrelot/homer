# Hephaestus

**Hephaestus** is the lame god of fire and the forge, son of Zeus and Hera, celebrated as the divine craftsman who forges Achilles' famous armour and, in the Odyssey, is the wronged husband of Aphrodite.

## Appearances

Iliad: appears notably in Books I, XVIII, XX, and XXI. Odyssey: appears notably in Book VIII.

## Personality

- **A peacemaker who tries to defuse quarrels among the gods**: "It will be intolerable if you two fall to wrangling and setting heaven in an uproar about a pack of mortals." (Iliad, Book I)
- **A master craftsman of unrivalled skill**: "would that I could hide him from death's sight when his hour is come, so surely as I can find him armour that shall amaze the eyes of all who behold it." (Iliad, Book XVIII)
- **A cunning inventor capable of devising subtle traps to avenge himself**: "he went to his smithy brooding mischief... and began to forge some chains which none could either unloose or break... Not even a god could see them, so fine and subtle were they." (Odyssey, Book VIII)
