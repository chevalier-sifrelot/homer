# Artemis

**Artemis** is the huntress goddess of the wild and twin sister of Apollo, who fights on the Trojan side in the Iliad's battle of the gods and is elsewhere invoked in both epics as the very image of beauty and grace.

## Appearances

Iliad: appears notably in Book XXI. Odyssey: does not appear directly, but is repeatedly invoked as a standard of beauty and swift death, notably in Books VI and XX.

## Personality

- **A fierce huntress and patroness of wild beasts**: "But his sister the huntress Diana, patroness of wild beasts" (Iliad, Book XXI)
- **Bold and taunting, quick to mock even her own brother for timidity**: "So you would fly, Far-Darter, and hand victory over to Neptune with a cheap vaunt to boot. Baby, why keep your bow thus idle?" (Iliad, Book XXI)
- **Proud but ultimately overmatched by stronger goddesses, reduced to tears when confronted**: "she fled weeping from under Juno's hand as a dove that flies before a falcon" (Iliad, Book XXI)

## Entourage

- Apollo
- Leto
