# Iris

**Iris** is the swift, rainbow-footed messenger goddess who carries Zeus's commands to gods and mortals alike throughout the Iliad, serving neither side of the war but the will of Olympus itself.

## Appearances

Iliad: appears notably in Books III, V, VIII, XV, and XXIV. Odyssey: does not appear.

## Personality

- **Swift and unwavering in her duty as messenger, "fleet as the wind"**: "Iris, fleet as the wind, obeyed him... even so did she wing her way till she came close up to the great shaker of the earth." (Iliad, Book XV)
- **Tactful and wise despite her subordinate role, capable of counselling even a great god**: "Sensible people are open to argument, and you know that the Erinyes always range themselves on the side of the older person." (Iliad, Book XV)
- **Skilled at approaching mortals gently, often adopting a disguise to soften her news**: "Iris went to Helen in the form of her sister-in-law" (Iliad, Book III)

## Entourage

- Zeus
- Hera
- Thetis
